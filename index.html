About

Who's keeping this archive.

I'm a Mathematics graduate from Lancaster University, where I focused primarily on real and complex analysis, measure theory, and functional analysis. More recently, I've gotten curious about the more applied side of maths — particularly where it overlaps with tech and programming. I have a lot of thoughts and ideas I like to share, so I started this blog as a place to do that — partly to keep track of what I'm reading and thinking about, instead of losing it all in scattered notes apps. On the rare occasion I'm not thinking about maths, tech, or science, I like to read, and explore new cafes and restaurants.
