About

I'm a mathematics graduate from Lancaster University, with abiding interests in analysis and probability. This page features mathematics I find beautiful and intriguing, as well as occasional reflections on learning.

I grew up on the slopes of Mt. Makiling, in the small town of Los Baños in the Philippines.

Pastimes

On the rare occasions I’m away from my whiteboard, I enjoy reading and exploring new coffee shops and restaurants. I’ve also recently started learning to ice skate. A dorky fact about me is that I love the anime Yuri on Ice, and after watching it (embarrassingly, several times), I became really interested in the sport.

On days I am feeling less inspired, I like to solve a few Project Euler and Leetcode problems. It can be pretty easy to lose motivation when these problems can be solved so easily now. But I don't think there’s any harm in simply enjoying the satisfaction of sitting down and figuring things out for yourself.
