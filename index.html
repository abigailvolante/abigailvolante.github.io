About

Who's keeping this archive.

I'm a Mathematics graduate from Lancaster University, where I focused primarily on real & complex analysis, measure theory, and functional analysis. After a while, I got curious about the more applied side of maths — particularly where it overlaps with tech and programming. I tend to have a lot of thoughts and ideas I like to share, so I started this page as a place to keep track of what I'm reading and thinking about, instead of losing it all in scattered notes apps. On the rare occasion I'm not thinking about maths, tech, or science, I like to read, and explore new cafes and restaurants.

N-01

Cauchy's Integral Formula

Complex Analysis — x min read

Is it possible to determine the value of a holomorphic function inside a region using only its values on the boundary? Cauchy's Integral Formula tells us that it is. Almost like magic. This remarkable result highlights just how rigid and unique holomorphic functions are in complex analysis.
