Books

A few books that have shaped my interests.

  • A Mathematician's Apology — G. H. Hardy. A must-read for anyone interested in mathematics. It perfectly captured how I felt about doing mathematics.
  • Fermat's Last Theorem — Simon Singh. The book that first got me properly interested in number theory.
  • How to Solve It — George Pólya. Still the best short thing written on how to actually attack a problem.
  • What Is Mathematics? — Richard Courant. One of the books that made me appreciate good mathematical exposition.
  • QED: The Strange Theory of Light and Matter — Richard Feynman. A great example of explaining difficult ideas clearly.
  • The Selfish Gene — Richard Dawkins. Changed how I think about optimisation outside mathematics.
  • Alex's Adventures in Numberland — Alex Bellos. The book I'd give to someone who thinks they're “not a maths person.”
  • The Code Book — Simon Singh. My first introduction to cryptography, from ciphers to public-key encryption.
