Starting a Blog

I've contemplated many times how I wanted this website to look since I wanted this site to be something that would come with me for many years, until the end of my career.

Initially, I felt that adding a blog section was rather too personal. A more curated and formal website seemed more acceptable. I worried I'd come across as weird if I wrote more than what was strictly necessary. However, I personally feel that we, as a society, would be better off writing more about our human experiences, especially now. I think this is what helps us connect as people. Finding someone whose experiences I can relate to has always made me feel less alone. So I hope this blog might give some readers that same feeling. At the end of the day, this site selfishly belongs to me, so I'd like to make it as personal and informal as I like.

Perhaps appropriately, I thought I'd start by writing about something I've been feeling lately.

Growing up, I always thought that the decisions of adults were the byproduct of intelligence, deliberation, and intentional action. I think this has probably contributed to how much I overthink things. I tend to assume that every decision must have a complicated reason behind it, when sometimes people are simply figuring things out as they go. My brain probably finds comfort in overanalysing things and assuming something is ten times more complex than it actually is.

My dad once described me as capable but mahina ang loob, roughly, someone who is sensitive and easily affected. He wasn’t entirely wrong. I can be quite sensitive, and I sometimes avoid situations where I feel I might not be as capable as the people in that room. I always feel like there are too many things I don't know. I do tend to come through in the end, even when I’ve spent far too much time worrying beforehand. That’s something I’m trying to remember now. Perhaps I need to give myself permission to be a little more confident.

Right now, I'm trying to get back to writing after a busy month.
